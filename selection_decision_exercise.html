8. Why BLUP is different

In a real breeding program, EBVs are not normally calculated by an arbitrary weighted average of “own BV” and parental EBV. Instead, a mixed model can use many sources of information simultaneously.

y = Xb + Zu + e
  • y = observed records
  • b = fixed effects such as contemporary group/pen
  • u = additive genetic effects to be predicted
  • e = residual effects

Key idea: BLUP predicts genetic merit using the animal's record and the information contained in relatives, pedigree, repeated records, progeny and other connected records.

Take-home: phenotype → adjustment for systematic effects → genetic prediction → accuracy → selection decision.
